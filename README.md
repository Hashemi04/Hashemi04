### Hi there 👋, I'm Seyed Mahdi Hashemi
**Frontend Engineer | Next.js & Nuxt.js**

I am a Frontend Engineer specializing in building large-scale logistics platforms, operational dashboards, and reusable component systems. I focus on modern meta-frameworks, strict typing, and SEO/performance optimization.

🚀 **What I do:**
* **Building at Scale:** Currently engineering end-to-end frontend solutions for platforms serving **over 300,000 customers and 200,000 registered drivers**. 
* **Design Systems & Architecture:** Experienced in building reusable UI systems, translating complex business requirements into scalable code, and improving Lighthouse performance metrics.
* **Leadership & Mentoring:** Serving as the Frontend Working Group Lead at my university, where I mentor aspiring developers and lead weekly technical sessions on software engineering principles.

💻 **My Tech Stack:**
* **Core:** JavaScript, TypeScript
* **Frameworks:** React, Next.js, Vue.js, Nuxt.js
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, SCSS, Shadcn, Bootstrap
* **State Management:** Pinia, Redux Toolkit
* **Testing & Tools:** Vitest, Jest, Cypress, Git, Docker
